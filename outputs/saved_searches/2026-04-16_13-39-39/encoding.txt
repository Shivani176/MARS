## Recent and Notable Papers on Positional Encoding:

### **Transformer and Language Model Focused:**

1. **"Linearized Relative Positional Encoding" (2023)** - Qin et al.
   - Proposes LRPE family for linear transformers
   - Maintains linear space-time complexity while preserving effectiveness
   - Shows SOTA performance in language modeling and classification

2. **"Rethinking Positional Encoding in Language Pre-training" (2021)** - Ke et al.
   - Identifies problems with absolute positional encoding in BERT
   - Proposes TUPE (Transformer with Untied Positional Encoding)
   - Separates word and positional correlations for better expressiveness

3. **"CoPE: A Lightweight Complex Positional Encoding" (2025)** - Amballa
   - Novel complex-valued encoding approach
   - Real part captures semantic content, imaginary part encodes position
   - Compatible with linear attention and shows superior GLUE performance

### **Vision and Spatial Applications:**

4. **"Conditional Positional Encodings for Vision Transformers" (2021)** - Chu et al.
   - CPE dynamically generated based on local neighborhoods
   - Generalizes to longer sequences than seen during training
   - Maintains translation-invariance for image classification

5. **"Positional Encoding as Spatial Inductive Bias in GANs" (2021)** - Xu et al.
   - Shows zero padding provides implicit positional encoding in GANs
   - Investigates alternative positional encodings for better spatial bias
   - Improves StyleGAN2 and SinGAN performance

### **Theoretical and General Approaches:**

6. **"Rethinking Positional Encoding" (2021)** - Zheng et al.
   - Broadens understanding beyond Fourier features
   - Shows performance determined by stable rank and distance preservation
   - Presents general theory for shifted basis functions

7. **"Novel positional encodings to enable tree-based transformers" (2019)** - Shiv & Quirk
   - Extends transformers to tree-structured data
   - Novel positional encoding for node positions in trees
   - 22% accuracy improvement on JavaScript to CoffeeScript translation

### **Specialized Applications:**

8. **"Training-free Spatially Grounded Geometric Shape Encoding" (2026)** - He
   - XShapeEnc for 2D geometric shapes
   - Exhibits invertibility, adaptivity, and frequency richness
   - Uses Zernike bases for shape geometry and pose encoding
