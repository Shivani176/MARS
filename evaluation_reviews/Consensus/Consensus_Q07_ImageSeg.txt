## **The Main Approaches to Image Segmentation**

Image segmentation means dividing an image into meaningful regions or objects. Methods can be grouped into **traditional** (pre–deep learning) and **deep-learning–based** approaches.

---

## **Classical (Pre–Deep Learning) Approaches**  

These use hand‑crafted rules on pixel intensity, color, or texture:

- **Thresholding & histogram-based**: choose one or more intensity thresholds (e.g., Otsu) to split foreground/background or multiple regions  (Minaee et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2024; Ghosh et al., 2019).  
- **Edge-based**: find strong changes in intensity, then link edges into object boundaries  (Xu et al., 2024).  
- **Region-based / region growing**: start from seed points and grow regions by similarity  (Minaee et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2024).  
- **Clustering**: group pixels into clusters (e.g., k‑means, hierarchical clustering) based on feature similarity  (Minaee et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2024).  
- **Graph-based & energy models**: formulate segmentation as graph cuts, Markov/conditional random fields, or active contours optimizing a global objective  (Minaee et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2024; Ghosh et al., 2019).

---

### Key Deep Segmentation Architectures

| Approach type | Core idea (very briefly) | Typical tasks | Citations |
|---------------|--------------------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Fully Convolutional Networks (FCN)** | Per-pixel classification using CNNs without fully connected layers | Semantic seg. |  (Minaee et al., 2020; Badrinarayanan et al., 2015; Ronneberger et al., 2015; Ghosh et al., 2019)|
| **Encoder–decoder (U-Net, SegNet)** | Downsample for context, upsample for precise boundaries, often with skip connections | Medical, natural images |  (Minaee et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2024; Badrinarayanan et al., 2015; Ronneberger et al., 2015; Bhatti et al., 2024)|
| **Multi-scale / pyramid & dilated conv (DeepLab)** | Capture context at multiple scales without losing resolution | Complex scenes |  (Minaee et al., 2020; Zhang et al., 2021; Ronneberger et al., 2015; Soylu et al., 2023)|
| **R-CNN-based instance seg. (Mask R‑CNN, etc.)** | Detect objects, then predict a mask per detection (top‑down) | Instance seg. |  (Minaee et al., 2020; Zhang et al., 2021; Sultana et al., 2020; Cheng et al., 2021)|
| **Attention / transformer & recurrent models** | Use attention or sequence modeling to refine pixel labels | Large scenes, long-range context |  (Minaee et al., 2020; Csurka et al., 2023; Kovtunenko & Mashtalir, 2025; Cheng et al., 2021)|
| **GANs / generative & hybrid models** | Use adversarial or mixed traditional+DL losses to improve realism or robustness | Medical, low‑label settings |  (Minaee et al., 2020; Xu et al., 2024; Taghanaki et al., 2019)|

---

## **By Segmentation Task**

- **Semantic segmentation**: label every pixel with a class (road, sky); often FCN, U‑Net, DeepLab, transformers  (Minaee et al., 2020; Csurka et al., 2023; Badrinarayanan et al., 2015; Ronneberger et al., 2015; Soylu et al., 2023).  
- **Instance segmentation**: separate each object instance (each person, each car); top‑down and bottom‑up CNN frameworks  (Zhang et al., 2021; Sultana et al., 2020).  
- **Panoptic segmentation**: unified view combining semantic + instance segmentation into one coherent output  (Zhang et al., 2021; Mohammed & Ralescu, 2024; Kirillov et al., 2018; Cheng et al., 2021).

---

## **Conclusion**

Main segmentation approaches evolved from simple thresholding, edges, regions, clustering, and graph models to powerful deep CNN encoder–decoder, multi-scale, and attention/transformer architectures, specialized for semantic, instance, and panoptic segmentation and widely used in natural, medical, and other imaging domains.
